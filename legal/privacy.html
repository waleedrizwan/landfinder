Privacy

Short version: no ads, no analytics, no tracking cookies, no accounts. The site keeps one login cookie and remembers a few choices in your own browser. Nothing is sold or shared.

Last updated 12 November 2026

All of these are needed for the site to work the way you asked it to, so there's nothing to opt into. The browser storage items never leave your browser. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes all of them.

The visitor count

The number in the header counts browsers, not people. To stop one browser being counted many times a day, the server keeps a one-way hash of your IP address mixed with a random value that changes every day. The IP address itself is never stored, and the hashes and daily values are deleted after two days, after which they can't be tied back to anyone.

Other services your browser talks to

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and the map library (cdnjs). Like any web host it sees your IP address and request details and may keep short-lived logs for security. Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • OpenStreetMap serves the map tiles when you open the map view. OSMF privacy policy.
  • Listing sites. When you click a listing you go to the site it came from (LandWatch, Zillow, Properstar and others). From then on their privacy policies apply, not this one.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, analytics, fingerprinting or social-media trackers.
  • No accounts, emails or forms beyond the password box.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of any data.

Your rights

Since the site holds nothing that identifies you, there's normally nothing to look up, correct or delete. If you have a question or a request anyway (including under the GDPR, UK GDPR or CCPA), ask the person who gave you access to the site.

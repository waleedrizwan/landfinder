Last updated 12 November 2026

Listings may be wrong

Every listing is gathered automatically from third-party sites and may be out of date, sold, withdrawn, mislabeled, a duplicate, a rental, or simply wrong. In particular:

  • Prices are estimates. Converted prices use exchange rates from the day the site was last built. Prices shown may leave out taxes, fees, commissions, transfer costs, back taxes, or the cost of an auction premium.
  • Sizes, locations and types are guesses. Acreage is parsed from listing text; map pins may sit at a ZIP code or town center rather than the property; property types and tags (ruin, castle, church…) come from simple keyword rules.
  • Translations are machine-made. Non-English titles and descriptions are translated automatically and can be inaccurate.
  • The listing site is the source of truth. Always check the original listing and the seller directly.

Not advice

Nothing on this site is legal, financial, tax, investment or real estate advice. Before buying any property, especially abroad, do your own due diligence: inspect it in person, check title and ownership, access and easements, zoning and building rules, flood and environmental risk, utilities, taxes, and whether foreigners may own land there. Use a licensed local lawyer, notary or agent. Be wary of scams: never send money to someone you haven't verified.
